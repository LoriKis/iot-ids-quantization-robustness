# Milestone 2 requirement checklist (post-run)

**Source of the requirements.** The official Milestone 2 assignment text is **not present** in this repository or in the two VM folders (only the official M1 checklist exists, in the
[M1 report](../milestone1-artifact/REPORT.md)). The items below therefore come from the review request for this branch, from the frozen M2 protocol
([`MILESTONE2_PROTOCOL.md`](source/v4-base/docs/MILESTONE2_PROTOCOL.md)) and from the overlay [`HANDOFF.md`](source/deadline-v1-overlay/HANDOFF.md). They are **not** a transcription of the official
requirements. Statuses: done, partly, deferred, not established. Evidence labels, as in the [report](REPORT.md): **recomputed from predictions** (also used for timing recomputed from raw measurement files), **confirmed from a config or log**, **reported but not independently verified**.

Distinctions this checklist keeps: the four measured models V1 to V4 are separate from the QAT feasibility pilot (rows 1 and 12); clean accuracy uses all 3,764 test rows while robustness uses the same 270 selected rows for every model (rows 4 and 5); one training seed is separate from three efficiency repetitions (rows 2, 8, 13); the exact VM source is separate from any post-run adaptation (rows 11 and 15).
