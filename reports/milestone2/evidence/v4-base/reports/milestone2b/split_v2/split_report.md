## Group-diversity criterion (algorithm version 2)

Revision 2 changes only the allocation. The dataset, grouping rule, scaler rule, acceptance band, hard row minima and feature-variability pins are those of revision 1. Identical feature vectors stay together, including groups with conflicting labels. The rule uses labels, group sizes and feature values only; no model output chose it.

For class c with G_c distinct feature-vector groups (a group counts toward every class among its rows' labels) the floor in partition p is `F_p(c) = min(max(absolute_floor_p, ceil(fraction_p * G_c)), floor(G_c / 3))`. Absolute floors: {'train': 5, 'validation': 5, 'test': 5}. The cap floor(G_c / 3) keeps each class individually satisfiable; for small G_c the train floor is the cap, not the fraction. The cap ignores groups shared by several classes, so joint satisfiability is decided by the search (V_div = 0), not assumed. The search stays lexicographic (V, J), with V = row shortfall + constant-feature risk + V_div and J = the version-1 deviation plus 1.0 * the mean squared deviation of the per-class group share from the partition targets.

Pre-registered ladder (order: for rung in ladder: for seed in seeds: first accepted wins). A rung is dropped before searching iff its structure-only row lower bound (sum of the F_p(c) smallest class-c group row counts, summed over classes) exceeds a band ceiling. **Chosen rung: 1**, seed 20270.

## Limitations

1. A class with a dominant feature vector still has most of its TRAIN ROWS from that single vector. The floor raises distinct vectors, not row balance. `split_comparison_v1_v2.md` reports per class the distinct vectors, the largest-group share in train and the effective vector count 1 / sum(p_g^2) over the class's train rows (reported only, not constrained).
2. Validation and test hold few rows for those classes (about 20-90), and the floors count distinct vectors, so per-class macro-F1 is noisy. Classes with fewer than 10 distinct vectors in validation or test are flagged as weak per-class evidence (revision 1 used 3).
3. Identical vectors with conflicting labels stay together and cap accuracy per partition (`conflicting_labels.json`).
4. Held-out coordinates outside the training range (for example the `Number` singleton rows) remain and are never clipped (`range_audit.json`).
